## 🧩 Description

An interactive browser-based electronic circuit simulator for analog, digital, and mixed-signal experiments, widely used for teaching, quick prototyping, and visual exploration of circuit behavior.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

<!-- FEATURES:START -->
- Interactive schematic simulation directly in the browser
- Covers analog, digital, logic, and mixed-signal examples
- Animated visualization of voltage, current, and dynamic circuit behavior
- Large built-in library of sample circuits for learning and experimentation
- Offline desktop packages available for Windows, Linux, and macOS
<!-- FEATURES:END -->
